# Additional Services, Top-ups and Payment Links: Sales Team Guide

*For: In-house Sales and iLeads team. No technical knowledge needed.*

---

## 1. The one rule

**Customers pay only through a payment link. The team does not enter payments by hand.**

There is no "Record payment" or "Add paid info" any more. You sell a service, send (or copy) the payment link, and the service turns **Paid** by itself when the customer pays through that link. Every payment therefore comes from the payment gateway and carries its own record.

From one screen called **Services & payments** you can:

1. **Sell another course** to an existing customer and send a **new payment link**, with no duplicate customer.
2. **Sell top-up classes** to a customer who finished their package.
3. **Sell a licence service** (for example a 2W licence) as its own item, with its own payment link.
4. **Copy** a link to paste yourself, or **resend** an old one.
5. See **every payment** with its unique **Payment ID** (like `PAY-3F9A2C71B0D4`) that you can copy and quote.

---

## 2. Words used in this guide

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Customer** | The person who bought from us. One customer = one profile. |
| **Service** | One thing the customer bought: a course, a set of top-up classes, or a licence service. A customer can have many. |
| **Payment link** | The link we send by email and WhatsApp so the customer can pay online. |
| **Payment ID** | `PAY-...` A unique code for each payment. One click copies it. |
| **Link ID** | `LNK-...` A unique code for each link we sent or copied. |

## 3. Where to open it

| You are on... | Do this |
|---|---|
| **Sales Dashboard**, click a tentative slot | In the Learner box press **Additional service** (it appears once the customer exists as a learner). |
| **Learner Management**, top card **"Existing customer: services & payments"** | Type the customer's **name or e-mail** and pick them from the list, or type a **Payment ID (PAY-...)** or a **Link ID (LNK-...)** to find a payment. You never need the phone number. |
| **Learner Management > Incomplete Payments** | Press **Additional service** on the customer's row. The row also shows the copyable Payment ID. |

The screen has two tabs: **Sell something** and **Services & payments (n)**.

## 4. Sell something

### 4.1 Another course
*Example: the customer has the 4W Beginner Course and wants another package.*

1. Click the **Another course** card.
2. Choose the **Course / Package**. The price fills in and you can change it.
3. Choose the **Payment plan**: Full, Half (50:50), or Custom (you type the amount due now).
4. Read the grey line: *"The link will charge Rs X now, then Rs Y later."* Check it is right.
5. Press **Create & send link** (email + WhatsApp/SMS go out) **or Create & copy link** (nothing is sent; the link is copied so you can paste it into your own WhatsApp chat).

The new service is added to the **same** customer. Earlier courses, payments and history are not touched.

| Message | What to do |
|---|---|
| "... is already fully paid for this customer." | They already own it. Pick a different one. |
| "This customer already has a pending ...". | It was added but not paid yet. Open the **Services & payments** tab and press **Resend**. Do not add it twice. |
| "...has no phone number" | The customer needs a phone number on file before a link can be built. Tell your team lead. |

### 4.2 Top-up classes
1. Click **Top-up classes**.
2. Enter the number of **Classes**. The price starts at **Rs 599 per class** and the total is worked out. You can change either.
3. Press **Create & send link** or **Create & copy link**.

A customer can have **one unpaid top-up at a time**. If you create another before they pay, the first is **updated**, not duplicated. You will see a message saying so.

### 4.3 Licence service (2W / 4W)
*Example: a customer enrolled for 4W and now also wants a 2W licence.*

1. Click **Licence service**.
2. Choose the **Vehicle** (2-Wheeler or 4-Wheeler), what it **Needs** (LL, DL, or LL + DL), and the **Fee**.
3. Read the grey line: *"The link will charge Rs X as one payment for this licence service."*
4. Press **Create & send link** or **Create & copy link**, exactly like a course or top-up.

The licence service appears as its own service (**"2W Licence: LL + DL"**). The customer pays the **whole fee in one payment** (no installments). When they pay, the service turns **Paid** and shows in their History. A licence service has no classes, so it has no **Book classes** button.

## 5. Services & payments tab

Every service the customer has is shown as a card:

- **Name and amount**, a status tag (**Unpaid**, **Half paid**, **Paid**), and the last link: *"link sent 2 h ago by Riya"* with its **Link ID**.
- **Copy link**: copies the payment link (and records that you shared it).
- **Resend**: sends the link again by email and WhatsApp/SMS.
- **Remove**: for a service added by mistake. It only works while **nothing is paid**, and asks you to confirm.
- **Book classes**: for courses and top-ups, see the booking guide.

Under each service you see its **payments**: Payment ID (click to copy), amount, date and status.

A service stays **Unpaid** until the customer pays through the link. If a customer says they paid but the service is still Unpaid, do **not** try to mark it paid. Check **Payment links sent** to confirm the link went out, ask them to retry the link, and tell your team lead if the money left their account. The tech team can look the payment up by its Payment ID.

Payments that cannot be matched to a service appear at the bottom under **Other payments**.

## 6. Finding a customer or a payment

In **Learner Management > Existing customer** type any of these:

| You have... | Type |
|---|---|
| The customer's name or e-mail | Type at least 3 letters, then **pick the customer from the list** (opens on **Sell something**). The list shows a masked number only to tell two people with the same name apart. |
| A Payment ID | `PAY-3F9A2C71B0D4` and press **Find** (opens on **Services & payments**) |
| A Link ID | `LNK-7C21A9F0B3` and press **Find** |

The Payment ID is also shown on every Incomplete Payments row. Click it to copy.

## 7. How everything connects

```
Customer
   +-- Service A (course)     -> Link LNK-... -> Payment PAY-... (paid through the link)
   +-- Service B (top-up)     -> Link LNK-... -> Payment PAY-...
   +-- Service C (2W licence) -> Link LNK-... -> Payment PAY-...
```

Every payment can be traced to: **who paid, which link, and which service.**

## 8. Do's and don'ts

**Do**
- Search for the customer first. Never create a second profile for the same person.
- Use **Create & copy link** when the customer is chatting with you on your own WhatsApp.
- Check the grey "The link will charge..." line before you send.

**Don't**
- Don't use **Edit** to sell a different service. Edit changes the customer's *existing* service. Use **Additional service**.
- Don't send the same service twice. Use **Resend**.
- Don't delete a learner to "start again". It permanently removes all their enrollments and payments. Use **Remove** on the one wrong service instead.

## 9. Common questions

**The customer says they did not get the link.**
Open **Services & payments**. Under the service you can see when and by whom the link was sent. Press **Resend**, or **Copy link** and send it yourself.

**The customer paid in cash or by bank transfer, how do I record it?**
You cannot. Customers pay through the link. Ask them to use the link; if there is a genuine exception, tell your team lead.

**Can I change the price?**
Yes, before you create the service. After a link is sent, use **Remove** (if unpaid) and create it again, or ask your team lead.

**Why can't I press Remove?**
Something has already been paid on that service. It cannot be removed.

**I see "Payment traceability is not set up".**
The tech team has not finished a one-time setup. Tell them.

**Will the customer see anything different?**
No. They get a normal payment link that opens straight to the service you chose.
