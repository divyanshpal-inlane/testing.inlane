# Editing and Deleting a Tentative Slot: Sales Team Guide

*For: Sales and iLeads team. No technical knowledge needed.*

A **tentative slot** is a hold you save on the Sales Dashboard for a customer (yellow). Click it to see its details in the left panel. This page explains who can **Edit** or **Delete** it and why.

---

## 1. Who can do what

| Who you are | Edit | Delete |
|---|---|---|
| **The person who created the slot** | Always (any payment status) | Always |
| **Any other team member** | Only while the slot is **Unpaid** | Never |
| **Super admin** | Always | Always |

*"Unpaid" is not typed in by anyone. It is the payment status of the service the slot is linked to (a slot with no service is Unpaid), so it changes by itself when the customer pays through the payment link.*

**Why?** An unpaid hold can be handed to a colleague (for example the customer is now paying through them). Once money is involved, only the person who owns the booking, or a super admin, should change or remove it.

The panel tells you in plain words why a button is missing, for example *"Created by Riya Shah, only they (or a super admin) can delete or edit this."*

| Panel note | Meaning |
|---|---|
| "Unpaid, anyone can edit this booking." | Any team member may edit it. Only the creator may delete it. |
| "Created by NAME, only they (or a super admin) can delete this." | You can still edit it if it is unpaid. |
| "No creator recorded..., only a super admin can delete it." | An old slot with no owner on record (for example one made in Instructor Management). |
| "Super admin, you can edit or delete this booking." | You are using your super admin override. |
| "Checking your account..." | Wait a moment. Buttons appear once the app knows who you are. |

---

## 2. Editing a booking

1. Click the yellow slot and press **Edit**.
2. The form opens with **every class booked together** (a multi-class booking is edited as one). Change the customer details, move a class to another time, remove a class or add one.
3. Press save.

**What is protected**
- You only ever load classes **you are allowed to edit**. If an older booking shares a customer with someone else's paid class, that class is not loaded.
- When you press save, the app **checks again** that nothing changed while the form was open. If Operations has **confirmed** a class meanwhile, someone deleted one, or the payment status or owner changed so you may no longer edit it, **nothing is saved** and you see a clear message. Close and reopen the booking.
- A confirmed class is never changed from here, even by a super admin.

---

## 3. Deleting a slot

1. Click the yellow slot and press **Delete** (only visible if you may delete it).
2. A confirmation appears: *"Delete the tentative slot for NAME? This can't be undone."*
3. If the booking has **more classes booked together**, you choose:
   - **Delete this class**: only the one you clicked.
   - **Delete all N classes**: the whole booking at once (only classes you are allowed to delete).
4. Press **Cancel** to keep everything.

**What is protected**
- The app checks the slot **again at the moment you confirm**. If it was already **confirmed by Operations**, already deleted, or no longer yours, it is **not** deleted and you see: *"This slot has changed since you opened it..."*
- Classes that could not be deleted are reported, for example *"1 class was not deleted (already changed or not yours)."*

---

## 4. Questions

**I created the slot but cannot delete it.**
Wait for "Checking your account..." to finish. If it persists, your name may have changed since you created the slot. Ask a super admin to delete it or tell the tech team.

**I want to hand my paid slot to a colleague.**
Only you (or a super admin) can edit it once it is paid. Ask a super admin.

**Two people have the same name, can one delete the other's slot?**
No. New bookings record the creator's **account**, not just the name, so a namesake cannot delete your slot.

**A slot disappeared after I pressed delete, can I get it back?**
No. Deleting is permanent. Create the slot again.

**Why can't I edit a slot Operations confirmed?**
It is no longer a hold. Ask Operations.
